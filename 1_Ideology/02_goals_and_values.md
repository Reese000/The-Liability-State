## Primary Goals

### 1. Eliminate Bureaucratic Entropy

**The Problem**: Departments grow just to manage the growth of other departments. Regulations accumulate, never expire, and create compliance burdens that crush small competitors while large corporations hire armies of lawyers to navigate them.

**The Solution**:
- Self-deleting laws with hard-coded expiration dates (10 years)
- Outcome-based funding (if results don't improve, budget cuts automatically)
- Competitive services (private sector can replace government departments if more efficient)
- A "House of Repeal" that can only remove laws, never add them

---

### 2. Create Incorruptible Governance

**The Problem**: Power concentrates. Those with power reward friends and punish enemies. Regulatory agencies are "captured" by the industries they regulate.

**The Solution**:
- Government as "Auditor" rather than "Ruler"
- The Strategist (executive) has influence but no treasury control
- Legislature selected by lottery (sortition) rather than election
- 75% supermajority required to change fundamental rules
- Private courts that resist lobbying (thousands vs. one agency)

---

### 3. Align Incentives with Outcomes

**The Problem**: In current systems, politicians benefit from appearing to solve problems, not actually solving them. Companies profit from socializing risk while privatizing gains.

**The Solution**:
- Citizens receive Dividend tied to national wealth (everyone benefits from growth)
- Decision-makers face personal liability (everyone suffers from bad choices)
- Insurance companies profit from safety (the regulator wants to avoid payouts)
- Inventors profit from actual use (royalties from licensing, not stock hype)

---

### 4. Preserve Merit and Social Mobility

**The Problem**: Generational wealth creates aristocracy. Trust fund children have no incentive to contribute. Class hardens over time.

**The Solution**:
- High inheritance tax (paid in equity, not forcing asset sales)
- Child Dividends locked until age 18 (no "baby factories")
- Every 18-year-old enters adulthood with a capital stake
- Status derived from reputation and contribution, not birth

---

### 5. Enable Post-Scarcity Flourishing

**The Problem**: As automation replaces jobs, people lose purpose. "What is the average human for?" becomes an existential question.

**The Solution**:
- The "Human Premium" economy (things machines can't do: empathy, handcraft, presence)
- Guild-based identity and community
- Frontiers (space, wilderness) for those who crave physical struggle
- Reputation capital replaces financial capital as primary status marker

## Core Values

| Value | Expression in the System |
|-------|--------------------------|
| **Freedom** | No permission required to act; only accountability after |
| **Responsibility** | Personal liability for decisions; no hiding behind corporations |
| **Transparency** | Glass-box government; all spending visible |
| **Efficiency** | Market mechanisms replace bureaucratic processes |
| **Meritocracy** | Wealth resets each generation; success is earned |
| **Community** | Guilds and regional compacts provide identity and support |
| **Prudence** | Unlimited liability encourages careful decision-making |

---

## What This System Rejects

| Rejected Concept | Reason |
|------------------|--------|
| Career politicians | Create perverse incentives for perpetual problems |
| Limited liability | Allows risk-taking with other people's money |
| Income tax | Punishes productive work |
| Standing armies | Concentrate violence in single entity; coup risk |
| Speculative stock market | Rewards hype over substance |
| Permanent bureaucracy | Grows without bound; resists reform |
| Centralized regulation | Subject to capture and lobbying |
