## The Transition Challenge

The Liability State is theoretically elegant, but the **transition** from the current system is the point of maximum danger. This section addresses:

- How to get from here to there
- What happens to existing institutions
- Realistic pilot programs to test concepts
- Specific adaptations for modern America

---

## The Core Problem

You cannot switch from a Bureaucratic State to a Liability State overnight.

**If you abolish regulations tomorrow**:
- Private courts won't have case law or speed to handle chaos
- Bad actors will loot the country in the gap
- Corporations dump toxic waste before liability sensors are active
- Warlords seize power before PMC bond market is liquid

## Principles for Transition

### 1. Gradual Handover

The government slowly retreats as the private sector proves it can take the load:
- 10-20 year "Regency Period"
- Parallel systems run simultaneously
- Old rules phase out as new rules prove viable

### 2. Prove Before Replace

Every government function must be **proven replaceable** before elimination:
- Private courts must demonstrate capacity
- Insurance markets must show coverage depth
- Guild certification must earn trust

### 3. Protect the Vulnerable

During transition, maintain safety nets until new systems are robust:
- Legacy welfare programs continue during phase-in
- Dividend system scales up over time
- No one falls through the cracks

### 4. Prevent Looting

Historical precedent (Post-Soviet Russia) shows elites will privatize assets into their own pockets:
- Strong anti-corruption enforcement during transition
- Transparent asset transfers
- Constitutional locks on key changes
