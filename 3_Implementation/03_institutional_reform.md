## Agency Transformation Guide

This document details what happens to major government agencies during transition.

---

## Agencies That Transform

### Internal Revenue Service (IRS)

| Current Role | New Role |
|--------------|----------|
| Collect income/payroll/capital gains tax | Collect LVT only |
| Massive compliance bureaucracy | Simple land assessment |
| Audits millions of returns | Audits property valuations |
| 80,000+ employees | ~5,000 employees |

**Transition**: As income tax phases out, IRS shrinks proportionally. Remaining function is land value assessment and collection.

---

### Environmental Protection Agency (EPA)

| Current Role | New Role |
|--------------|----------|
| Write regulations | Collect pollution fees |
| Inspect facilities | Audit Environmental Trusts |
| Enforce compliance | Manage extraction royalties |
| Thousands of inspectors | Fee collectors and auditors |

**Transition**: Shift from regulation to pricing. Pollution fees replace emission limits. Environmental Trusts sue for actual harm.

---

### Food and Drug Administration (FDA)

| Current Role | New Role |
|--------------|----------|
| Pre-market approval | Post-market liability |
| Years-long approval processes | Insurance-driven safety |
| Government certifies safety | Private certifiers compete |

**Transition**: FDA becomes one of many certification bodies. Liability insurance provides real safety incentive. Sandbox zones for experimental drugs.

---

### Occupational Safety and Health (OSHA)

| Current Role | New Role |
|--------------|----------|
| Workplace safety regulations | None (abolished) |
| Inspector visits | Insurance auditors |
| Fines for violations | Liability for harm |

**Transition**: Full transfer to insurance market. Workers' comp insurers become the safety police. Direct financial incentive for safety.

---

### Department of Education

| Current Role | New Role |
|--------------|----------|
| Federal education mandates | None (abolished) |
| Student loan programs | Education Wallet vouchers |
| Testing standards | Guild certification |

**Transition**: Education fully privatized. Vouchers follow students. Guilds set standards. No federal involvement.

---

### Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD)

| Current Role | New Role |
|--------------|----------|
| Housing subsidies | Dividend provides housing money |
| Public housing management | Private/municipal ownership |
| Community development grants | LVT funds local development |

**Transition**: Functions transfer to municipalities and markets. Dividend replaces housing assistance.

---

## Agencies That Dissolve

| Agency | Reason | Replacement |
|--------|--------|-------------|
| **SEC** | No stock market | Bond/royalty market self-regulates |
| **FTC** | Insurance ceilings prevent monopoly | Market forces |
| **FCC** | Spectrum sold/leased | Property rights in spectrum |
| **TSA** | Airline liability | Private security + insurance |
| **DEA** | Drug liability** | Private consequences |
| **ATF** | Weapon liability | Insurance and courts |

**Note on drugs**: In a liability state, drug harm is handled through courts. Manufacturers liable for addiction/damage. Personal use is personal risk.

---

## Agencies That Remain

| Agency | Retained Role |
|--------|---------------|
| **Department of Defense** | Auditor of PMC bonds, munitions escrow |
| **Department of State** | Diplomacy (reduced scope) |
| **Treasury** | National Trust, currency, LVT collection |
| **Department of Justice** | Court certification, appeals |

## Creating New Institutions

### National Trust

**Function**: Manages all public revenue and Dividend distribution

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| LVT Collection | Assess and collect land value tax |
| Fee Management | Pollution, extraction, spectrum fees |
| Dividend Distribution | Equal payments to all citizens |
| Escrow Services | PMC bonds, munitions locks |
| Bounty Administration | National prize payouts |

---

### Private Court Certification Board

**Function**: The "ISO" for courts

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Certification | Approve courts for operation |
| Standards | Due process requirements |
| Monitoring | Track fairness metrics |
| Decertification | Remove corrupt courts |

---

### Defense Coordination Council

**Function**: Coordinate fragmented military without commanding it

| Role | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Bounty Posting | Issue defense bounties in conflict |
| Contract Management | Maintain tripwire contracts |
| Intelligence Sharing | Coordinate threat detection |
| Escrow Administration | Manage munitions locks |

## Personnel Transition

### What Happens to Government Workers?

| Category | Transition Path |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Inspectors/Auditors** | Hired by insurance companies, certifiers |
| **Administrative staff** | National Trust or private sector |
| **Specialists** | Guild employment |
| **Enforcement** | Private security, PMCs |
| **Redundant positions** | Severance + Dividend cushion |

### Transition Support

- Multi-year severance packages
- Retraining programs
- Dividend provides safety net
- Private sector absorbs regulatory talent
