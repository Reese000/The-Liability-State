## Governance Design

The political structure is designed to prevent tyranny through **separation of Authority (power to punish) from Influence (power to lead)**.

---

## The Executive: The Strategist

### Role

A "Chief Visionary Officer" with **Influence, not Authority**.

| Has | Does Not Have |
|-----|---------------|
| Power of the Pulpit (speeches, vision) | Legislative power (cannot write laws) |
| Power to Propose National Missions | Command of police (private) |
| Cultural leadership | Treasury control |
| Diplomatic representation | Ability to seize money |

### How National Missions Work

1. **Strategist Proposes**: "We need to go to Mars. It will cost $1 Trillion."
2. **Cannot Take Money**: Must initiate a National Referendum
3. **Citizens Vote**: If shareholders approve, funds unlock
4. **Spending Controlled**: Private Courts unlock specific funds for that mission only

**Result**: Strong leader energy (rallying cry, strategic direction) without capacity for tyranny (no checkbook, no gun).

### The Bonded Executive (Emergency Powers)

**The Problem**: Democracies fail when enemies attack faster than voters can deliberate. But "emergency powers" create dictators.

**The Solution**: The Strategist can activate emergency powers **by posting a personal liability bond**.

| Phase | Mechanism |
|-------|-----------|
| **Trigger** | Strategist declares "Imminent Threat" |
| **Bond Posted** | Personal assets (home, savings, pension) locked as collateral |
| **Powers Granted** | Temporary authority to mobilize defense, bypass referendum |
| **Duration** | 90 days maximum, renewable once |
| **Audit** | After crisis: Private Courts audit decisions |

**If Justified**: Bond returned, leader honored.

**If Abuse/Error**: Bond forfeited to victims. Strategist personally liable for excess spending.

**Why This Works**:
- **Deters Fake Crises**: You don't fabricate emergencies if your house is collateral
- **Enables Speed**: Real crises get instant response
- **Accountability**: No "fog of war" immunity—every dollar justified post-hoc

### The "Recall" Switch

- **House of Repeal** can trigger immediate "Vote of No Confidence"
- Because Strategist has no army or treasury, losing means simply... fired
- No leverage to stage a coup

## The Executive Oversight Panel (Sortition Panel of 3)

### Purpose

A check on the Strategist's residual powers — not to govern, not to make policy, but to **block** specific classes of executive action that carry high damage potential if abused. Functions as a hard override switch on a constitutionally-defined list of actions.

### Selection: Weighted Sortition

| Requirement | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **Active dues-paying guild member, currently employed in trade** | Excludes retirees, the idle rich, and anyone disconnected from current economic reality. Ensures panelists have live skin in the game |
| **Not receiving guild retirement or pension benefits** | Verifiable, objective proxy for retirement status. Retirees have different incentives — less to lose, less current context |
| **5+ years active guild membership in the last 7** | Ensures continuous, recent economic engagement. Prevents lapsed members from rejoining just to qualify |
| **No guild executive or board-level leadership in the last 5 years** | Prevents guild management from leaking into the panel. The panel draws from rank-and-file working members, not governance insiders |
| **Clean liability record (10 years)** | No outstanding Harberger judgments, fraud settlements, or rehabilitation contracts |
| **Pass civic literacy assessment** | Objective test administered by the Actuarial Commons: Three-Source Mandate, Option Premium, guild mutual insolvency mechanics. No essay, no ideology — right/wrong answers only. Available year-round, self-administered, open to all |
| **No elected office ever held** | No career politicians |

**Pool composition**: All citizens who meet the above criteria are entered into the lottery pool. Every citizen can self-qualify by taking the assessment.

### Term and Staggering

- **6-month term**, no reappointment, permanently ineligible after service
- **Staggered start dates**: one member drawn every 2 months
  - This ensures no panel of 3 is ever fully new at the same time — there is always a 2-month overlap
  - Prevents "recruit a whole panel at once" corruption scenarios
  - To corrupt the panel, an actor needs to target one member every 2 months over 6 months
- **Confirmed draw**: 6 names are drawn; the 3 serve after a 2-week public confirmation window. Any of the 6 can withdraw (time, health, etc.) and the next name from the pool replaces them. The ones who stay are the ones who *want* to serve

### Powers (Constitutionally Defined)

The panel can **veto** — by a 2/3 supermajority — the following classes of executive action:

| Executive Action | Normal Use | Abuse the Panel Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Emergency declaration** | Earthquake, invasion, pandemic | Expands state power indefinitely; uses emergency to entrench |
| **Guild mutual de-certification** | Insolvent guild poses systemic risk | Political revenge against a guild that opposed the executive |
| **Treaty / foreign commitment** | Trade agreement, mutual defense | Sells out citizens' liability protections; commits to foreign adventures |
| **Snap referendum call** | Genuinely urgent constitutional question | Exploits temporary crisis in public opinion |
| **Harberger estate forced sale** | Tax delinquency, public necessity | Expropriates political opponent's land at declared price |

The panel has **no power to initiate** anything — only to block. Everything else the executive does unilaterally.

### Excluded Powers (Deliberately Withheld)

The following powers were considered and explicitly rejected for the panel. Their absence is a deliberate design choice:

| Rejected Power | Reason Excluded |
|---|---|
| **Calling audits or investigations** | The panel has no institutional expertise, no ongoing relationship with the auditing ecosystem, and no skin in the game for audit costs. Audit-calling power would allow the panel to impose costs on entities it dislikes without bearing those costs. Auditing is handled by the market through insurer inspections, Trust Broker certifications, adversarial audit bounties, and Private Civil Servant warrants. The panel should not reintroduce a single-point-of-pressure bottleneck into a decentralized system. |
| **Initiating legislation or policy** | The panel is a check on the executive, not a legislature. Only the House of Repeal can remove laws; only the Strategist can propose them. |
| **Appointing or removing officials** | The panel cannot hire or fire anyone. Personnel decisions remain with the executive or the relevant guild/corporation. |

The panel's power begins and ends with blocking specific classes of executive action. Any proposal to expand panel powers requires a constitutional amendment with 75% House of Repeal supermajority plus ratification by a majority of Regional Compacts.

### Pardon Power

The panel owns the pardon — not the executive. By unanimous vote (3/3), the panel can issue a binding release from a private liability judgment. The executive can recommend a pardon; only the panel can grant one.

This is a last resort for cases where the statutory whistleblower protections do not apply but the result is clearly contrary to the public interest. The high threshold — unanimous agreement among three randomly selected citizens — prevents abuse while preserving an escape valve for genuine injustice.

### Recurring Functions (Preventing Idle Irrelevance)

1. **Certify Actuarial Commons model updates** — Every 3 months, sign off on updated risk tables. Mechanical, non-political, keeps the panel engaged and trained in the system's core logic.

2. **Publish State of the Constitution report** — At the end of each term, a simple public report: actions reviewed, vetoes cast, gaps found in the system. This is the panel's legacy and gives the citizenry a reason to follow their work.

### The Talent Pipeline Effect

A 6-month term on the Oversight Panel is a proving ground for future Bonded Executives. A panelist who serves well has:
- Public visibility for their judgment
- Deep system-level knowledge
- A track record of decisions (all public)
- No ability to game the system (one term only)

To become Bonded Executive, a former panelist must still compete in a general election. Their panel record is their reputation capital — nothing more, nothing less.

### Why 3

- More personal and visible than a larger body — citizens can follow individual members
- Overwhelms the executive less — a block from 3 people carries more weight than a block from 15
- Small enough that each member's reputation is personally at stake
- Vulnerable to unlucky stacks (mitigated by the confirmed draw with 6 drawn, 3 serve) and political capture (mitigated by staggering), but the trade-off is worth it for focus and accountability

## The Legislature: House of Repeal

### Selection: Sortition

Members are selected by **random lottery**, like jury duty.

| Elected Legislature | Sortition Legislature |
|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Career politicians | Ordinary citizens |
| Lobbying targets | Impossible to corrupt in advance |
| Polarized parties | Statistically representative |
| Fundraising dependency | No campaign finance |

### Function: Negative Power Only

The House of Repeal **cannot pass laws**. It can only:
- **Repeal** existing regulations
- **Fire** corrupt Auditors
- Trigger **Vote of No Confidence** on exectuvies

### The 75% Rule (Supermajority Lock)

To prevent mob rule:
- Changes to the Dividend formula require 75% consensus
- Changes to basic rights require 75% consensus
- Simple 51% majority cannot rule

### The Constitutional Lock (Class Warfare Prevention)

**The Problem**: What stops citizens from voting 100% tax to steal from corporations, or corporations from lobbying for 0% tax to starve the people?

**The Solution**: The LVT rate is **constitutionally locked**.

| Protected Element | Change Requires |
|-------------------|-----------------|
| LVT rate formula | 75% supermajority |
| Dividend distribution formula | 75% supermajority |
| Core rights definitions | 75% supermajority |

### The Zero-Sum Budget Incentive

The formula is strict: **Tax Revenue - Government Costs = Citizen Dividend**

| If Citizens Want | They Must Accept |
|------------------|------------------|
| More programs | Smaller dividend checks |
| Bigger dividend | Smaller government |

**Effect**: Every citizen becomes a ruthless auditor of government waste. Unlike current systems where "Free Money" is desirable, in the Liability State, every cent of waste is a cent removed from the individual's pocket. The classes are unified by a shared goal: **Systemic Efficiency**.

## The State Layer: Regional Compacts

### The Need for Hierarchy

Humans are tribal—we crave "Teams." Being "from Georgia" or "from Texas" means something. Erasing regional identity creates a cultural vacuum.

### What States Become

Instead of governments that pass laws daily, States become **Standardized Legal Frameworks**:

| Traditional State | Regional Compact |
|-------------------|------------------|
| Taxes individuals | Invoices member Cities |
| Writes new laws | Maintains "flavor" of existing law |
| Permanent bureaucracy | Can be dissolved by member Cities |
| Political rulers | Cultural custodians |

### The "OS" Model

Different Compacts have different "flavors":

| Compact | Flavor |
|---------|--------|
| **Georgia Compact** | Agricultural traditions, community cohesion, maker culture |
| **Nevada Compact** | High-risk finance, gambling freedom, loose restrictions |
| **Texas Compact** | Energy independence, minimal interference |

**Effect**: People self-sort to regions matching their values.

### Functions of Regional Compacts

1. **Cultural OS**: Default values for courts settling disputes
2. **Megaproject Union**: Board for regional infrastructure (rail, rivers)
3. **State Guard**: Volunteer militia for disaster relief
4. **Regional Identity**: Holidays, monuments, State Games
| **Circuit Breaker** | Nullify federal overreach |
| **Commons Stewardship** | Manage shared regional resources |

### Compact Differentiation

Compacts differentiate on the following five axes, each of which creates a distinct legal and cultural environment:

| Axis | What Varies | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Damage valuation** | How courts value non-economic damages (pain, suffering, environmental loss) | Kansas compact caps non-economic damages at $500K; Coastal compact at $5M |
| **Environmental harm definition** | What counts as pollution, resource depletion, or ecological damage | Mountain compact classifies groundwater depletion as strict liability; Gulf compact prices it within a resource extraction framework |
| **Guild authority** | How much autonomy guilds have over certification and standards | Texas compact delegates medical licensing entirely to the Medical Guild; Vermont requires dual guild-compact certification |
| **Court preference** | Default rules for court selection | One compact mandates random-rotation court assignment (anti-capture); another allows contractual choice (efficiency-focused) |
| **Risk tolerance** | What activities are permitted and at what mandatory coverage levels | Nevada compact allows high-risk finance with correspondingly high coverage minimums; New England compact sets lower risk ceilings |

All compacts must operate within the constitutional floor: no ex-ante licensing for most activities, recognition of private court judgments across compacts, free movement of citizens and capital, and the requirements of the Three-Source Mandate.

### Compact Boundaries

The Liability State is designed to allow experimentation with different political and economic systems within its borders, subject to constitutional constraints:

| Ideology | Can it exist within a compact? | Limitation |
|----------|-------------------------------|------------|
| **Minarchist / market-dominant** | Yes | This is the default setting |
| **Social democratic** | Partial | No income tax (constitutionally banned), but compacts can add local taxes on top of LVT or fund public services through municipal subscriptions |
| **Communist (state-owned production)** | Partial | The compact could own factories as corporate entities, but those entities must carry insurance, face liability, operate as Glass Boxes, and cannot claim sovereign immunity |
| **Theocratic (religious law)** | Partial | Religious courts can operate alongside liability courts, but liability judgments must be enforceable. Where a religious ruling would deny a victim compensation that liability law requires, the liability court prevails |
| **Authoritarian (strong executive, weak courts)** | **No** | Private courts must be independent; insurers must be able to challenge government actions |
| **Hyper-bureaucratic (extensive ex-ante licensing)** | **No** | The constitutional commitment to ex-post liability prohibits compacts from imposing broad ex-ante licensing regimes |

**The force prohibition:** No compact may raise a military, police force, or armed militia for use against other compacts or against residents exercising their constitutional rights. All defense is national (War Escrow / PMC system). Inter-compact disputes are resolved through **private courts by default** (compacts agree on a neutral venue). If a compact refuses to comply with private court rulings, or if a compact violates the force prohibition, the dispute escalates to the **Supreme Trust Oracle** — the backstop enforcement layer with power to dissolve rogue compacts and trigger the Trust Blockade.

### Commons Stewardship

One of the critical gaps in a pure market-liability system is the problem of shared resources: fisheries, watersheds, airsheds, aquifers. These are not owned by any single party, so no single liability suit can resolve their depletion or degradation.

The Liability State addresses this through the **Regional Compact** layer, not through federal regulation. The National Alliance explicitly has no regulatory power over commons management.

| Feature | Design |
|---------|--------|
| **Regulatory level** | Compact only. The federal level may not regulate natural resources, land use, or environmental standards. |
| **Scope** | Each compact defines "reasonable use" of shared regional resources — fisheries, watersheds, air quality, mineral rights, aquifer extraction |
| **Enforcement** | Through the liability system. A compact that permits over-exploitation of a shared resource can be sued by downstream compacts for the harm caused. |
|| **Inter-compact commons** | For resources that cross compact boundaries (major river systems, migratory fisheries, atmospheric pollution), compacts enter into mutual agreements. Breach of such an agreement goes through the private court system by default. If a compact refuses to comply, the dispute escalates to the **Supreme Trust Oracle** for enforcement. |
| **Competitive pressure** | A compact that mismanages its commons loses residents and businesses to compacts with better stewardship. Exit is the ultimate check on regulatory failure. |

### Accountability

- Compacts are funded by **invoices to member Cities**
- If Compact becomes bloated/corrupt, Cities vote to **Dissolve and Reform**
- Leadership must stay efficient or lose "customers"

## The National Alliance: Enumerated Powers

The Alliance is a strictly enumerated service provider, not a sovereign. Its powers are limited to four functions, each with a constitutional mandate, a time-limited charter, and no authority to expand beyond its scope.

### The Four Functions

| Function | Description | Funding Source |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Inter-Compact Dispute Resolution** | Supreme Trust Oracle — adjudicates disputes between compacts, enforces cross-compact judgments, issues Freedom of Exit Determinations for external conquest review | Alliance skim |
| **Border Defense** | Contracts Private Military Companies for territorial defense against foreign threats. Coordinates intelligence and screening. May not fund offensive operations — those are separate War IPO mechanisms (see [Defense §Offensive Operations](./07_defense_military.md#offensive-operations-the-war-ipo)) | Alliance skim + War IPO |
| **Currency & Land Value Index** | National Trust administers the LS Dollar peg, publishes the daily Land Value Index, maintains uniform valuation methodology across all compacts | Alliance skim |
| **Citizen's Dividend Floor** | Calculates and publishes the constitutional minimum Dividend amount (pegged to subsistence in the cheapest habitable area). Audits compact compliance. Compacts may supplement but not reduce. | Alliance skim |

**The Alliance has no power to:**
- Impose taxes of any kind (income, sales, VAT, corporate)
- Regulate commerce, land use, environment, labor, or any economic activity
- Mandate compact-level policies or standards beyond the constitutional floor
- Maintain a standing army (all military capacity is private PMC, contracted per-need)
- Issue debt that binds compacts
- Create new government departments or agencies beyond the four functions

Any spending beyond the four functions is ultra vires and is subject to lawsuit by any compact in the STO. Alliance officials who authorize ultra vires spending are personally liable for the amount.

### Funding: The 10% Skim

The Alliance is funded by a **constitutional skim of 10% of compact-level LVT revenue**:

| Parameter | Value | Rationale |
|-----------|-------|-----------|
| **Rate** | 10% of each compact's LVT revenue | High enough to fund Alliance functions and build rapid war credit, low enough that even full spending cannot cripple the economy |
| **Collection** | Automatic lockbox — compacts remit directly to a constitutionally protected trust. Compacts cannot withhold or delay. | Prevents compacts from leveraging the Alliance by starving its budget |
| **Reallocation** | No discretion — trust disburses to the four functions by formula (not by Alliance choice) | Prevents the Alliance from picking winners among its own functions |
| **Refund** | All unspent balance at fiscal year end is refunded **proportional to each compact's contribution** → flows back to that compact's Citizen Dividend pool | Creates constant pressure for efficiency; any waste is money that does not return to citizens |

**Why proportional refund, not per-capita:** The Alliance is a service provider, not a redistribution engine. Compacts that pay more receive more back when the Alliance underspends. This is consistent with the minimal Alliance philosophy — the Alliance has no mandate to transfer wealth between compacts.

**Why 10% and not lower:** In peacetime, most of the 10% will be refunded, creating strong compact scrutiny of any Alliance spending. In crisis (a war bond), the skim provides immediate capital credibility — insurers and investors see a 10% standing revenue stream backing Alliance commitments, which makes Victory Bonds more credible.

### Sunset Mechanism

Each Alliance function has a **10-year charter** that must be re-authorized by a majority of Regional Compacts to continue:

| Phase | Action | Effect |
|-------|--------|--------|
| **Year 0** | Compact ratifies the constitution | Alliance established with four functions, 10-year clock starts |
| **Year 8** | STO conducts a performance review of each function | Published report: spending, outcomes, waste, efficiency |
| **Year 10** | Each compact votes separately on each of the four functions | Function dies if majority of compacts do not re-authorize |
| **If a function dies** | Its residual budget is divided among compacts proportional to their contributions | No Alliance function is permanent; all must earn renewal |

A function may be re-authorized for another 10 years, modified (e.g., scope reduced), or terminated. A function may also be terminated early by a 75% supermajority of the House of Repeal plus ratification by a majority of compacts.

**Why sunset matters:** Without it, the Alliance's four functions would be permanent regardless of performance. The sunset clause forces compacts to periodically evaluate whether each function is still worth funding. It also prevents function creep — if the Alliance tries to expand its scope beyond the four functions, compacts can let the charter expire and negotiate a narrower replacement.

### Accountability Summary

| Mechanism | What it prevents | Response time |
|-----------|-----------------|:------------:|
| **Enumerated four functions** | Mission creep | Immediate (lawsuit for ultra vires) |
| **10% skim + automatic refund** | Hoarding, empire-building | Annual (refund) |
| **Proportional refund** | Redistribution through Alliance | Annual |
| **10-year sunset** | Permanent bureaucracy | Decade, can be accelerated |
| **Compact audit rights** | Wasteful spending within functions | On demand (STO complaint) |
| **Personal liability for officials** | Authorizing unauthorized spending | Immediate (private court suit) |

## Transparency Mechanisms

### The Public Ledger

All government spending tracked in real-time:
- Every cent from treasury to contractor visible
- No "slush funds" (math must balance)
- Open-source legislation (every change logged with author)

### The Glass Box State Mandate

The Alliance and all Regional Compacts must operate with **Glass Box Transparency**:
- **Real-Time Treasury Log**: Every single cent that leaves the state treasury—from defense bounties to court maintenance—is visible on the public ledger within 60 seconds of transaction.
- **Model Transparency**: Any formula used to calculate the **LVT** or the **Citizen's Dividend** must be open-source and auditable.
- **External Audit Access**: Any individual or group can trigger an "Adversarial Audit" of a state function. If the audit discovers waste or fraud, the auditor is paid a bounty from the recovered funds (Incentivized Vigilance).

### Temporal Transparency (The Vault Model)

Military and diplomatic secrets are kept secret in real-time BUT:
- Automatically unlock after set period (2-5 years)
- Politicians cannot block "unsealing"
- Leaders won't commit crimes if evidence will be public while they're alive

### Zero-Knowledge Auditing

Using cryptographic proofs, government can prove statements without revealing data:

**Example**: Military proves to public auditor:
- "We spent $1B on fuel"
- "The money was actually spent on fuel"
- WITHOUT revealing: Chemical formula or refinery location
