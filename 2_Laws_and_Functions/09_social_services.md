## The Citizen's Dividend as Subsistence Floor

### Not a Salary—A Floor

The Dividend is **not** designed for comfortable living. It is calculated to provide a fixed national amount:
- 2,000 calories/day
- Basic shelter
- In the **cheapest habitable area** of the entire system

This amount is the same for every citizen, regardless of where they live. The number is recalculated periodically against the current cheapest county to account for inflation and shifting population.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Universal** | Everyone receives it, regardless of employment |
| **Subsistence-Level** | A fixed national number — covers survival in the cheapest area, not comfort everywhere |
| **Geographic Arbitrage** | Want to live in an expensive city? Must work. Comfortable on the dividend alone? Move to a rural area. The amount is the same everywhere — its purchasing power varies by location. |
| **Non-Distorting** | No welfare cliff; working always adds income |
| **Self-Adjusting Target** | The floor is pegged to the cheapest habitable area, not to any county permanently. As people move to cheap areas and raise costs, the cheapest area shifts — preventing any single "welfare county" from forming. |

### The "Hunger Gap" Incentive

| Location | Dividend Covers | Incentive Effect |
|----------|----------------|-----------------|
| Cheapest rural area (e.g., rural Alabama) | Rent + food + utilities | Can survive without working |
| Mid-cost area (e.g., Atlanta) | Maybe rent OR food, not both | Must work for comfort |
| High-cost city (e.g., San Francisco) | Almost nothing | Strong incentive to work or move |

**Effect**: The dividend is not generous enough to support a desirable lifestyle in expensive areas. It creates a gradient: the closer you want to be to economic opportunity, the more you need to work. But no one starves, and the rural countryside is repopulated organically.

## Disability Care: The Genetic Lottery Pool

### Accident-Caused Disability

If you're disabled due to someone's negligence:
- Insurance pays immediately
- Tort settlement covers lifetime care + lost wages
- Courts enforce full compensation
- Actually **better** than current system

### Congenital/No-Fault Disability: No Bureaucrat Gatekeeping

**The Problem**: Traditional systems require a bureaucrat to decide who is "disabled enough."

**The Solution**: The Genetic Lottery Pool

| Step | Mechanism |
|------|----------|
| 1. Funding | Fixed % of LVT revenue goes to High-Risk Reinsurance Pool |
| 2. Trigger | Three independent, randomly-selected physicians diagnose |
| 3. Consensus | If all 3 concur on diagnosis code, coverage activates automatically |
| 4. Fraud Check | Colluding doctors face fraud liability + license revocation |

**Key Insight**: It's a contract, not a favor. No caseworker decides your fate.

### What Coverage Includes

| Need | Coverage |
|------|----------|
| Medical care | Market-rate for diagnosed condition |
| Support equipment | Patient chooses provider |
| Living assistance | Based on independence level |

**Status**: Customer with purchasing power, not beggar dependent on budget allocation.

## Unemployment Insurance

### Mandatory Mutual Aid

Like car insurance, unemployment insurance is **mandatory**:

- Lose job → Private insurer pays you
- Chronically unemployed due to laziness → Premiums spike
- Eventually dropped → Must rely on (smaller) Dividend
- Incentive to stay employable

## Education: The Guild Track

### Abolish Traditional Public School

Public school was designed to train factory workers. In post-scarcity, we train **Masters, Explorers, and Creators**.

### The Education Wallet

- Every child receives dedicated Dividend portion
- Can **only** be spent on education
- Follows the child, not the school

### Micro-Schools

| Traditional School | Micro-School |
|-------------------|--------------|
| 2,000 students | 15 students in a workshop |
| Standardized curriculum | Specialized learning |
| One-size-fits-all | Physics taught through rebuilding engines |

### Early Apprenticeship

Around age 12-14, children can "bid" their Education Wallet to join a Guild:
- Machinists
- Coders
- Medics
- Scientists

### The Masterpiece Graduation

| Traditional Graduation | Liability State Graduation |
|-----------------------|---------------------------|
| Pass scantron test | Produce a Masterpiece |
| Credential = attendance | Credential = demonstrated skill |
| Easy to fake | Judged by Guild Elders |

**Examples**:
- Build a functional robot
- Write a novel
- Machine a precision turbine

If it fails, you don't graduate. Try again.

## Child Protection: The Abuse Bounty

### The Problem

Child abuse is hidden — the victim cannot report it, and the state lacks the distributed eyes needed to detect it.

### The Mechanism

| Step | Description |
|------|-------------|
| 1. **Report** | A mandatory reporter (teacher, doctor, guild master, neighbor with documented proximity) or Trust Broker-endorsed claimant files a suit |
| 2. **Investigation** | Private court investigates, with expedited timeline |
| 3. **Verdict** | If abuse/neglect is substantiated, the court orders the parents' assets or insurer to pay |
| 4. **Bounty** | A portion of the award goes to the claimant (court discretion, published as precedent) |
| 5. **Restitution** | Remaining funds go to child relocation, therapy, and housing |
| 6. **Placement** | Court determines the child's best option — family, guild sponsorship, or private care paid from the award |

### Anti-Fraud Protections

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| **False reports for bounty** | Loser Pays rule applies. Claimant must post a lowered litigation bond. Cap bounty at a moderate amount (e.g., $50K per substantiated claim + costs) — enough to incentivize reporting, not enough to incentivize framing. |
| **Wealthy parents as targets** | Court discretion on bounty percentage (within guidelines). Published precedent prevents systematic over-awarding. |
| **Cultural variation in "abuse"** | Regional Compacts define abuse standards as part of their legal "OS" |
| **Gaming by parents** | Child Dividend is already locked in trust. Parents have no financial incentive to have children for profit. |

### Coverage for Unsubsidized Care

If no family member or guild will take the child, the court orders care from a private service, paid from the parents' liquidated assets or insurance. The child becomes a **customer with purchasing power**, not a ward of the state.
