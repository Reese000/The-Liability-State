## The Ambulance Chaser Problem

In a system driven entirely by lawsuits, the biggest danger is that the legal system itself becomes a **weapon for extortion**. This section covers mechanisms to prevent abuse.

---

## Mechanism 1: Loser Pays (English Rule)

### Current US System

Anyone can sue anyone for free. Even if the defendant wins, they pay their own expensive lawyers. This encourages "settlement extortion"—paying $5k to make a nuisance suit go away because fighting costs $50k.

### Liability State Fix

**Strict English Rule**: If you sue and lose, you pay 100% of the defendant's legal bills.

**The Math for a Scammer**:
- Potential upside: Maybe $20k settlement
- Potential downside: $100k+ in corporate legal fees
- Risk/Reward: Terrible

**Result**: Frivolous suits stop immediately.

---

## Mechanism 2: Litigation Bonds

### The Spam Filter

Before a judge will look at your case, you must post a **Litigation Bond** (or get an insurance company to back you).

**How It Works**:
1. Plaintiff wants to sue for slip-and-fall
2. Court requires $10k bond (or Legal Insurance)
3. If case is obviously fraudulent, no insurer will back it
4. Without bond, court rejects the case

**Effect**: Acts as a "filter" preventing baseless claims from clogging the system.

### Access to Justice Concern

**Problem**: Poor people with legitimate claims can't afford bonds.

**Solution**: Trust Brokers / Litigation Financiers
- Specialized firms back legitimate claims
- They take a percentage of the eventual payout
- Strong cases find backing; weak cases don't

## Mechanism 3: Reputation Databases

### The Boy Who Cried Wolf

Private courts and insurers maintain a shared **Plaintiff Database**.

**Tracking**:
- Number of suits filed by individual
- Win/loss record
- Types of claims (slip-and-fall, workplace injury, etc.)
- Payout history

**Impact on Serial Plaintiffs**:
- "John Doe" has slipped in 4 grocery stores in 2 years
- His "Credibility Score" hits zero
- On the 5th claim, jury is shown his history
- Burden of proof shifts entirely to him

**Effect**: Blacklisted from "easy payout" market.

## Mechanism 4: Forensic Surveillance

### The Black Box Store

In a liability-based world, businesses defend themselves aggressively with technology.

**Standard Provisions**:
- High-resolution cameras with timestamps
- Floor sensors logging spills and cleanups
- Environmental monitors
- Activity logs

**Defensive Use**:
- Scammer claims puddle was there for an hour
- Store produces sensor log showing floor was dry 30 seconds ago
- Video shows scammer pouring water from own bottle

### The Counter-Suit

Once fraud is proven:
- Store doesn't just win—they **sue the scammer**
- Charges: Fraud, Defamation, Perjury
- Scammer faces financial ruin
- Creates powerful deterrent effect

## Mechanism 5: Comparative Negligence

### Shared Responsibility

Instead of "the corporation is always guilty," courts assess **relative fault**.

**Example: Wet Floor Case**

| Current System | Liability State |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Store 100% liable: floor was wet | Floor was wet AND plaintiff was running while looking at phone |
| Full payout | 80% Comparative Negligence on plaintiff |
| | Reduced or zero payout |

### Cultural Shift

From: "The Corp is always guilty"
To: "Watch where you are going"

**Effect**: Reduces payout potential significantly when plaintiff contributed to their own harm.

### Fair Balance

A reasonable court might rule **50/50 liability**:
- Store should have cleaned the spill
- Customer should have been paying attention
- Payout reduced by 50%

---

## Mechanism 6: Professional Licensing

### Attorneys as Gatekeepers

Attorneys are personally liable for frivolous suits:
- Required to certify cases have merit
- False certification = malpractice
- Insurance companies monitor attorney track records

**Effect**: Lawyers won't take cases they can't win—their livelihood depends on it.

---

## Summary: The Deterrent Stack

| Layer | Mechanism | Risk to Scammer |
|-------|-----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Loser Pays | Pay defendant's legal fees |
| 2 | Litigation Bond | Lose deposit |
| 3 | Reputation Database | Future claims flagged |
| 4 | Forensic Evidence | Case demolished by data |
| 5 | Counter-Suit | Criminal fraud charges |
| 6 | Attorney Gatekeeping | Can't find lawyer to take case |

**Combined Effect**: Filing a fake lawsuit is as dangerous as committing robbery.
