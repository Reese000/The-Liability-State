## Infrastructure as Service

In the current system, government builds roads using tax money. In the Liability State, infrastructure is treated as a **Service, not a Right**.

---

## Roads: Subscription Services

### Ownership Structure

| Type | Owner |
|------|-------|
| **Highways** | Large Logistics Guilds |
| **Local Streets** | Neighborhood Trusts (residents) |
| **Bridges** | Construction/Maintenance Guilds |

### Payment Model

Instead of gas tax, pay a **Mobility Subscription** (like Spotify for roads).

### The Pothole Guarantee

**Liability-Driven Maintenance**:
- If a pothole damages your vehicle, the road owner is **strictly liable**
- Road owners are obsessed with maintenance
- Fix problems at 3 AM to avoid claims
- Competition drives quality

### The "Landlocked" Solution

**Concern**: What if a neighbor buys the road and blocks you in?

**Answer**: Easement of Necessity
- Common Law dictates property cannot be sold without access
- Every property has inherent "Right of Way"
- Road owner must permit passage for reasonable fee
- Cannot legally barricade residents

## The Grid: Power & Water

### Natural Decentralization

Because liability for massive blackouts is astronomical, the grid **naturally fragments**.

**The Math**: If one company owns the entire grid, a single disaster could bankrupt the insurance sector. Therefore...

### Micro-Grids

- Neighborhoods run their own solar/generators
- Factories maintain backup power
- Connection to main grid is for **backup only**
- Power company becomes a "battery backup service"

### Water Systems

Similar decentralization:
- Local water trusts own treatment/distribution
- Competition on quality and reliability
- Liability for contamination drives investment in safety

## Property Rights: The Bubble Interaction

### The "Quiet Enjoyment" Standard

You don't just own the dirt—you own the **peace** on that dirt.

| Violation | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| Neighbor blasts music at 2 AM | Bill them for "Loss of Quiet Enjoyment" |
| Factory emits foul smell | Sue for property damage |
| Construction creates dust | Compensation for cleaning/health |

**Enforcement**: If they refuse to pay, private court places lien on their property.

### Visual Covenants

**Handled by Contract, Not Zoning**:
- When you buy a house, you sign a deed covenant
- Example: "I agree not to paint my house neon pink"
- Voluntary contract, not government mandate
- Breach = breach of contract → lawsuit from neighbors

### The "Good Samaritan" Exception

To prevent a cold, heartless society, courts recognize **Emergency Privilege**:

| Scenario | Ruling |
|----------|--------|
| Trespassing to escape fire | Not liable for trespass (life > property) |
| Broke fence while escaping | Still owe for fence repair |

**Principle**: Preserve life, compensate for damage.

## Public Spaces: Trust-Managed Commons

### Do "Public" Spaces Exist?

Yes, but structured differently:

| Current System | Liability State |
|----------------|-----------------|
| Government owns parks | Trusts own parks |
| Anyone can use, anyone can trash | Trustees liable for safety |
| "Tragedy of the Commons" | Incentive to maintain |

### Trust Structure

- Parks, squares, wildlands owned by **Private Trusts**
- Trustees have fiduciary duty to maintain
- "Public" = anyone can use
- "Private" = trustees can exclude troublemakers
- If park becomes dangerous, trustees face lawsuits

### Environmental Protection

**Who Sues for the Ocean?**

Instead of government "owning" rivers:
- Rivers placed in environmental trusts
- Trustees have fiduciary duty to water quality
- Company dumps waste → Trust sues for property damage
- Replaces vague "environmental fines" with specific claims

## Society Structure: The "Contract" Culture

### The Biggest Cultural Shift

Today, we interact as "Citizens" (subject to same rules). In the Liability State, we interact as **Contractors**.

### Entering Private Jurisdictions

- Walk into a mall → entering private jurisdiction
- Sign at door: "By entering, you agree to arbitration for disputes"
- Digital identity auto-negotiates terms

**Your Settings**: "I only agree to ISO-Certified arbiters"
**Store Settings**: "We only use Certified Arbiters"
**Result**: Phone pings "Agreement Signed," door opens

### Municipal "Subscription"

| Traditional City | Municipal Corporation |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| You live there, you're subject to laws | You "subscribe" to services |
| Taxes mandatory | Fees for services used |
| No exit option | Can move to different provider |

**Services Included**: Trash pickup, park maintenance, local roads, neighborhood security
