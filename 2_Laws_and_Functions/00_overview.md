## Key Interactions

### The Feedback Loop

1. **Economic Activity** generates demand for land
2. **Land Value** increases from development
3. **LVT Revenue** captures that value for the public
4. **Citizen's Dividend** distributes wealth to all
5. **Consumer Spending** fuels more economic activity

### The Safety Loop

1. **Business** wants to operate
2. **Insurance** required to get coverage
3. **Insurer** inspects for safety
4. **Unsafe Practices** → Unaffordable premiums
5. **Safe Operation** or **Shutdown**

### The Justice Loop

1. **Harm** occurs
2. **Victim** files suit (with litigation bond)
3. **Private Court** adjudicates
4. **Loser** pays all legal fees
5. **Precedent** set for future cases

### The Evolution Loop

*The Liability State has no legislative body that creates new regulations. The system updates itself through the following cycle:*

1. **Knowledge advances** — scientific discovery, technology, or cultural change reveals a new type or source of harm
2. **Standard of care shifts** — after knowledge is available, continuing the harmful behavior falls below the standard of reasonable care
3. **Liability attaches** — courts find actors liable for failing to adapt to known risk
4. **Insurers price the shift** — premiums increase across the affected industry
5. **Behavior changes** — the harmful practice becomes more expensive than the alternative, without any regulation being passed or law being written

The Evolution Loop is faster than legislation (no regulatory lag, no comment periods, no political capture) and self-sunsetting (when risks recede, liability recedes naturally). It is the system's primary adaptation mechanism.

### The Trust Loop (Private Civil Servants)

*The Glass Box Mandate requires corporate transparency, but legitimate secrets (proprietary algorithms, trade secrets, security infrastructure) cannot be made public. The Private Civil Servant system bridges this gap:*

1. **Probable cause** — a whistleblower, insurer, or the Actuarial Commons presents evidence of possible violation to a private court
2. **Warrant issued** — the court reviews evidence ex parte and issues a warrant if probable cause is found
3. **Deep audit** — 2-3 bonded Private Civil Servants from independent networks inspect the secret data
4. **Attestation** — the PCSs issue a public verification without revealing the underlying secrets
5. **Consequence** — if violation found, the company faces liability; PCS corruption risks personal ruin from sureties

The Trust Loop allows market-based auditing of the most sensitive corporate functions without requiring public disclosure of legitimate secrets or creating a bureaucracy of government inspectors.

### The Enforcement Cascade (Inter-Compact Disputes)

*Inter-compact disputes are resolved through the private court system by default. Only when private courts cannot complete enforcement does the Supreme Trust Oracle intervene:*

1. **Dispute arises** — two compacts disagree over a shared resource, liability ruling, or regulatory boundary
2. **Private court** — the compacts agree on a neutral private court venue. The court issues a fair resolution
3. **Compliance** — in 95%+ of cases, the losing party complies voluntarily (or the Good Faith Bond covers the judgment)
4. **Escalation** — if a compact refuses to comply, or if forum shopping produces irreconcilable judgments, the dispute escalates to the Supreme Trust Oracle
5. **STO enforcement** — the Trust Blockade invalidates the rogue compact's land titles across all compacts, or the Good Faith Bond is liquidated

This preserves the private ordering principle while ensuring the system has a backstop against rogue compacts.
