## The Market-Based Border

Borders in the Liability State are not guarded by government agents but priced by **insurance markets** and enforced by **liability chains**.

---

## Citizenship: The Pay-to-Play Model

### Citizenship Bond

To become a citizen, a foreigner must pay a **Citizenship Bond** — a one-time fee that funds the Public Liability Pool for risks introduced by new entrants.

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Fee** | Variable, adjusted to maintain target immigration flow |
| **Financing** | Can be paid via loan (guild sponsorship as collateral) |
| **Insurance Sponsorship** | Must be endorsed by a certified insurer who underwrites the immigrant's liability for a probationary period |
| **Children** | Immigrant children enter the Child Trust system automatically upon parent's citizenship |

### Insurance Sponsorship Requirement

Every immigrant must have **liability coverage from a certified domestic insurer** before entering the country:

1. Insurer vets the immigrant (background check, financial standing)
2. If approved, insurer posts a **Surety Bond** covering the immigrant's liability for 5 years
3. If the immigrant causes uncompensated harm, the insurer pays
4. If the insurer fails to vet properly, they absorb the full cost

**Effect**: Insurers become the border guards. They have financial incentive to keep out high-risk entrants and admit low-risk ones. No walls, no TSA — just insurance pricing.

## Temporary Visitation

Visitors enter on a **time-allotted pass** priced by the market:

| Model | Mechanism |
|-------|-----------|
| **Per-day pass** | Cost covers liability insurance for duration of stay (e.g., $X/day + insurance underwriting) |
| **Duration** | Fixed term (e.g., 90 days). Renewable from outside country. |
| **Overstay** | Automatically uninsured → cannot rent housing, work, or engage in commerce |
| **Conversion** | Can convert to citizenship by paying remaining bond difference |

## Illegal Immigration

### The Liability Gap Problem

An illegal immigrant has no insurance, no registered address, and no legal identity. They introduce **unaccounted liability** into every area they operate in:

| Activity | Problem |
|----------|---------|
| **Renting housing** | Landlord rents to uninsured tenant → landlord is strictly liable for any damages the tenant causes (cannot pass liability to tenant) |
| **Employment** | Employer hires an uninsured worker → employer is strictly liable for all worker's actions (no workers' comp shield) |
| **Commerce** | Any business transaction with an uninsured person carries unlimited liability exposure |

### The Market Response

It is **illegal to do business with an uninsured person** in any capacity where liability could be transferred:

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Renting to uninsured tenant | Landlord fully liable for all tenant damages (no recourse) |
| Employing uninsured worker | Employer fully liable for all worker actions |
| Selling goods/services | Seller liable for any downstream harm |

**Effect**: The market excludes illegals more efficiently than any border patrol. An illegal cannot rent an apartment, buy a car, get a job, or sign a phone contract — not because of government enforcement, but because doing business with them is financially suicidal for the other party.

## The Sponsorship Loop (Guild-Driven Immigration)

Guilds can sponsor immigrants, creating a natural labor flow:

1. Guild determines it needs more workers in a trade (shortage)
2. Guild sponsors a foreigner: pays their Citizenship Bond + provides insurance coverage
3. Immigrant gets citizenship and a job
4. Immigrant repays the guild through wage garnishment or a service contract
5. If the immigrant reneges, the guild is insured against the loss

**Why this is self-balancing**: If a guild has a genuine labor shortage, they pay the premium to import workers. If domestic workers are available, importing is more expensive than hiring locally. No central planning needed — the price signal handles it.

## Border Security

### Physical Border

The border is **not** a government-controlled checkpoint. It is a **liability boundary**:

- Infrastructure within the border is insured
- Infrastructure outside the border is not
- Entering the country without insurance means you are an uninsurable liability — any interaction with you carries risk
- Private border security firms exist, but their contracts are bought by insurers and guilds, not the state

### Asylum and Humanitarian Cases

| Case | Mechanism |
|------|-----------|
| **Refugee** | Humanitarian Sponsor Bond: a guild or Trust Broker sponsors the refugee's liability for 5 years. The sponsor is liable for damages. |
| **Stateless person** | Assigned Risk Pool covers their liability at standard rates. Regional Compacts rotate responsibility. |

The system does not recognize a right to enter without sponsorship — but it also does not build walls. The liability chain enforces the boundary.
