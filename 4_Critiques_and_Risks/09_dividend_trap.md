# The Dividend Trap (Revenue Hunger)

[← Back to Overview](./00_overview.md) | [Index](../README.md)

---

## The Risk: Externalizing for Profit

Because the Citizen's Dividend is strictly tied to (**Tax Revenue - Costs**), citizens have a direct financial incentive to prioritize short-term revenue over long-term sustainability or ethical concerns.

---

## The Mechanism

### 1. The Pigouvian Paradox
Pollution fees are intended to discourage pollution. However, if these fees fund a large portion of the Dividend, the "shareholders" (citizens) may oppose regulations that actually succeed in *reducing* pollution, as it would shrink their checks.

### 2. Resource Exhaustion
Citizens may vote (via the House of Repeal or Compact level) to accelerate resource extraction (mining, logging) to "top up" the dividend during economic downturns, creating a tragedy of the commons authorized by the victims.

### 3. "Sin" Monetization
The state may become dependent on fines from anti-social behavior, leading to "over-policing" of liability-generating activities to maintain fiscal levels.

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: Dividend Smoothing Fund
Divert a percentage of surplus into a multi-generational sovereign wealth fund (The Permanent Trust) to decouple monthly payouts from volatile fee revenue.

### Mitigation 2: Externality Vaults
Pollution fees could be "time-locked" or restricted to environmental restoration only, removing them from the Dividend pool to eliminate the perverse incentive to pollute.

### Mitigation 3: Constitutional Fee Caps
Limit the percentage of the Dividend that can come from non-LVT sources (e.g., no more than 20% from pollution fees/fines).

---

## Organic Resolution

The Dividend Trap is resolved through **Liability-Adjusted Dividends** and **Health Insurance Feedback**:
- **Health-Risk Correlation**: If a citizen votes to allow a polluting factory in their Compact to increase the **Citizen’s Dividend**, their health insurance premiums will rise by more than the dividend gain. This is because the private insurer is directly liable for the increased medical costs associated with the smog or toxic groundwater.
- **The Risk Offset**: The system ensures that the "Profit" from environmental extraction is instantly offset by the "Cost" of personal liability. 
- **The Externality Vault**: Any revenue from "High-Impact" extraction is mandated by the **Supreme Court of Liability** to be held in an restoration escrow, removing the cash from the monthly dividend pool and killing the incentive for citizens to "Loot" their own biosphere for a quick check.
