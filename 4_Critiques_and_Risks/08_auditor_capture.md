## The Risk: Auditor Capture and Institutional Decay

The Liability State relies on **certifiers** (private) and **court-certifiers** (state) to maintain the integrity of the trust market. If these entities are captured, the entire system of accountability collapses.

---

## The Mechanism of Capture

### 1. The Expert Bottleneck
Complex industries (nuclear, bio-engineering) have few qualified auditors. If all experts are hired by the industry they audit, "independent" certification becomes an inside job.

### 2. The Certification Cartel
A few large certification firms (like the big credit rating agencies) could dominate the market, creating a "too big to fail" or "too big to sue" situation where their stamps of approval are mandatory but meaningless.

### 3. State-Level Auditor Corruption
The minimal state function of certifying private courts is a prime target for lobbying. If the government certifying body is captured, it can whitelist biased courts and blacklist honest ones.

| Phase | Vulnerability | Impact |
|-------|---------------|--------|
| **Entry** | Industry sets the certification standards | Barriers to entry for new firms |
| **Operation** | "Pay-to-play" for favorable reports | Systemic risk hidden from insurers |
| **Judgment** | Auditors provide favorable expert testimony | Courts misled on technical liability |

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: Adversarial Auditing
Incentivize "Bounty Auditors" who are paid (via court awards) to prove a previous certification was negligent.

### Mitigation 2: Auditor Liability Link
If a certified facility causes harm, the **Auditor is personally liable for a percentage of damages**, equivalent to the builder.

### Mitigation 3: Sortition Auditing Board
The state body that certifies courts should be overseen by a **sortition-selected jury of citizens** (House of Repeal subcommittee) to prevent long-term lobbying capture.

### Mitigation 4: Open-Source Audit Protocols
All certification standards must be public and repeatable by any competitor.

---

## Organic Resolution

The Auditor Capture problem is resolved through **Adversarial Certification** and **Liability Bond Forfeiture**:
- **Bounty Auditing**: If Auditor A "rubber stamps" a dangerous facility, Auditor B (a competitor) is incentivized to issue a **"Risk Alert"**. If Auditor B is proven right via an Alliance-ordered safe-start audit, they are awarded 50% of Auditor A's **Professional Liability Bond**.
- **The Reputation Suicide**: In a market where "Trust" is the primary currency, an auditor caught taking a bribe is permanently blacklisted by all **Liability Insurers**. 
- **The Cross-Pillar Check**: Because insurers are liable for the failures of the auditors they trust, the insurers themselves act as the "Police of the Auditors." They will ruthlessly litigate against any certifier who provides false data, as it directly threatens the insurer's solvency.
