## The "Post-Selection Corruption" Problem

While sortition (random selection) makes the House of Repeal "impossible to corrupt in advance," it makes individual members highly vulnerable **after** they are selected.

---

## The Mechanism

### 1. Extreme Intimidation
A regular citizen is suddenly given the power to repeal a regulation that costs a billion-dollar insurance cartel millions. The cartel doesn't need to lobby; they just need to "inform" the citizen of the risks to their family's reputation or personal stability (via private investigators).

### 2. The Golden Parachute
Instead of a bribe today, the member is offered a "high-status guild position" or $10M partnership interest that begins exactly one day after their term ends.

### 3. Informational Capture
Citizens are generalists. They rely on "experts" to explain the laws they are repealing. In the Liability State, these experts are often employees of the industries or insurance companies being debated.

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: The "Hush" Sortition
Members of the House of Repeal work in anonymity. Their names are not public until their term is over. They live in "The Cloister" (a high-security, high-luxury compound) during their 1-2 year term.

### Mitigation 2: Post-Service Pension
Every sortition member receives a massive, life-long "Independence Pension" that is forfeited if any private income is received for 10 years after service.

### Mitigation 3: Adversarial Experts
The State must fund "Counter-Experts" for every industry proposal, ensuring sortition members hear two competing, well-funded lies rather than one.

---

## Organic Resolution

The Sortition Corruption is resolved through **Redundant Deliberation** and **The Cloister Protocol**:
- **Multi-Group Redundancy**: Critical votes in the **House of Repeal** are assigned to three independent, anonymous groups simultaneously. If Group A votes differently than Groups B and C, a "Discrepancy Audit" is triggered, leading to the manual investigation of Group A’s **Liability Bonds** for signs of bribery.
- **The Independence Pension**: Every sortition member is granted a lifetime "Independence Stipend" from the **National Trust**. This stipend is large enough to ensure that the marginal utility of a bribe is near-zero, and the penalty for taking one (total forfeiture of the stipend and all personal assets) is absolute.
- **Market Vigilance**: Because citizens know that a corrupted vote will shrink their **Citizen’s Dividend**, they act as a "Distributed Stasi," monitoring for any sudden wealth accumulation in their neighbors who have recently served.

---

## Bottom Line

| Risk | Mitigation | Residual Risk |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Intimidation | Anonymity (Cloister) | Low |
| Bribery | Independence Pension | Medium |
| Brainwashing | Adversarial Experts | Medium |

Randomly selecting people is a great way to start a legislature, but protecting them while they serve is the harder, unresolved challenge.
