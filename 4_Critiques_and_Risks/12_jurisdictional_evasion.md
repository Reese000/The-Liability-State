## The Risk: Jurisdictional Evasion (Capital Exile)

The Liability State works because "you can run but you can't hide" your assets from a court. But what happens if the assets move to a jurisdiction that doesn't recognize Liability State rulings?

---

## The Mechanism

### 1. The offshore Shadow
A company operates in the Liability State but holds its core capital, IP, and "Executive Penthouses" in a traditional Westphalian state (e.g., a modern tax haven). When a $50B liability hit occurs, the company simply declares bankruptcy in the Liability State and continues operating from the offshore shadow.

### 2. Asset Liquidity Delay
In the time between a "Violation Detected" and a "Judgment Rendered," digital assets (crypto, royalties) can be moved across borders in seconds.

### 3. The "Shell" Partnership
Using complex layers of non-member partnerships to mask the "Executive Pillar," making it impossible for private courts to find whose assets to "pierce."

### 4. Internal Secession (The Dead Zone)
An ideological compound or micro-state (e.g., an Anarcho-Capitalist enclave) purchases land and legally declares "Zero Accountability"—meaning no insurance, no courts, and no liability inside their fence, effectively evading Liability State jurisdiction from within.

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: The "Entry Bond"
To do business in the Liability State, a non-resident entity must escrow a "Surety Bond" proportional to their potential systemic risk. No bond = No license.

### Mitigation 2: Universal Reciprocity Bounties
The Alliance issues "Asset Recovery Bounties." If a judgment evader flees, PMCs or Repo-Bounty Hunters are authorized to seize their assets anywhere in the world (high risk of international incident).

### Mitigation 3: KYC/KYA (Know Your Assets)
The "Public Ledger" must include the **Ultimate Beneficial Owner** of all land and partnerships. Hidden ownership = Automatic forfeiture of LVT status.

### Mitigation 4: The Jurisdictional Quarantine (Economic Siege)
The system permits "Zero-Accountability Dead Zones" via pure consent, but relies on absolute economic isolation. No global insurer or bank will process transactions with a Dead Zone. It becomes a quarantined petri dish, permitted to experiment internally but strictly banned from exporting external risk.

---

## Organic Resolution

The Jurisdictional Evasion is resolved through **Asset-Linked Identity**, **Bounty Seizure**, and **Liability Firewalls**:
- **The Presence Requirement**: You cannot own property or hold a **Harberger Land Title** in the Liability State without a local **Surety Bond** parked in a certified bank. If an entity flees (Capital Exile), this bond is immediately liquidated to satisfy victims.
- **The Global Repo-Market**: The Alliance issues "Asset Recovery Bounties" that are 200% of the judgment value. This incentivizes world-class PMCs to track down and seize the offender’s offshore assets. 
- **The Corporate Death Penalty (Economic Siege)**: Any entity found evading a judgment, or any "Dead Zone" that externalizes uncompensated harm across its border, is blacklisted from the **Global Trust Ledger**. This makes it impossible to get insurance, banking, or utilities anywhere in the Alliance. Rogue states and enclaves are financially quarantined; they must conform to Liability rules at the border to survive, causing radical experiments to burn out locally rather than metastasizing systemically.
