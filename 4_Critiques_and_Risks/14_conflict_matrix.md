## The "Gridlock" Problem

In a polycentric system with no central authority, what happens when the core pillars (Insurers, PMCs, Courts, the Strategist) disagree on a fundamental reality?

---

## The Resolution Matrix

| Conflict | Primary Arbiter | Secondary Check | Resolution Mechanism |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Insurer vs. Court** | Market Force | The Strategist | Insurer moves to different court; Strategist audits court's certification if patterns of bias emerge. |
| **Court vs. PMC** | Bounty Market | Insurance Cartel | Court issues judgment; if PMC ignores it, Insurers fund a competitor to "Liquidate" or "Sieze" the rogue PMC's assets. |
| **Strategist vs. House of Repeal** | Referendum | Private Courts | Strategist proposes; HoR repeals. If standoff, a national vote (Shareholder Meeting) resolves the strategic direction. |
| **Citizen vs. Insurance Cartel** | Trust Brokers | Public Option | Citizen uses specialized Trust Broker to find "Guild Mutuals" or enters "Assigned Risk Pool." |
| **Compact vs. Alliance** | Supremacy Clause | Regional Veto | Issues involving national defense/currency go to Alliance; internal cultural/land issues stay with Compact. |

---

## The "Ultimate Circuit Breaker" (The Chaos Protection)

If the matrix fails and the system enters a "Looping Conflict" (e.g., Two courts claim jurisdiction over the same $100B asset and both PMCs are mobilizing):

1. **The Strategist** declares a "Semantic Halt."
2. **All Escrows** are frozen for 72 hours.
3. **The High Trust Council** (A sortition-selected group of past-term HoR members) hears the 3-hour emergency case.
4. **Binding Verdict** is issued.
5. **Enforcement** handles by the "Alliance Guard" (A rotating, non-market military unit funded by 1% of LVT with NO offensive capability).

---

## Bottom Line

The Liability State is not a "No-Government" system; it is a "Distributed Government" system. The Matrix ensures that every conflict has a clear path to resolution before the first bullet is fired.
