# Military Risks (The Wagner Effect)

[← Back to Overview](./00_overview.md) | [Index](../README.md)

---

## The Risk: Mercenary Coups and Extortion

The risk is that private military companies (PMCs) realize they hold the monopoly on violence and use it to extort the state or seize power directly.

---

## The Mechanism

### 1. Market Monopolization
If one PMC achieves >50% market share, the "Competitive Bounty" system fails. The PMC can demand "Triple Bounties" during a crisis or threaten to stand down unless political demands are met.

### 2. Conflict Manufacturing
PMCs have a profit incentive to ensure war never ends. They may secretly fund "managed rebels" to sustain high bounty prices and keep the "Iron Vault" open.

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: The Logistics Firewall
Legally mandate the separation of "Combat PMCs" (tanks/soldiers) from "Logistics PMCs" (fuel/ammo). A coup attempt runs out of gas in 72 hours without the cooperation of a competitor.

### Mitigation 2: The Traitor's Bounty
If a PMC goes rogue, its "Surety Bond" is instantly converted into a "Liquidate Bounty" for all its competitors. Greed keeps the market stable.

### Mitigation 3: Citizen Militia (The Porcupine)
Maintain an armed, trained populace. PMCs can win a battle, but they cannot occupy a hostile, armed nation profitably.

---

## Organic Resolution

The Wagner Effect is resolved through **Bountied Loyalty** and **Logistical Fragmentation**:
- **Multi-Vendor Defense**: The National Alliance maintains a "Polycentric Defense Market." No single PMC is allowed to hold more than 30% of the strategic logistics (fuel, ammunition, or intelligence) of a region. This **Logistics Firewall** ensures that a coup runs out of fuel in 72 hours if the separate fuels-provider refuses to cooperate.
- **The Traitor’s Bounty**: A massive portion of the PMC’s **Surety Bond** is held in a "Betrayal Escrow." If a PMC attempts to overthrow the Compact, any soldier or rival PMC that "Liquidates" the rebel leader is paid that bond instantly. 
- **The Liability Checkmate**: Because PMCs must be insured to operate legally, any PMC that shows signs of political ambition sees their **Liability Premiums** skyrocket. This effectively "Taxes" the coup out of existence before it can begin, as the cost of insurance becomes higher than the expected profit of tyranny.

---

## Bottom Line

| Risk | Mitigation | Residual Risk |
|------|------------|---------------|
| PMC Extortion | Logistics Firewall | Medium |
| Coup Attempt | Traitor's Bounty | Medium |
| Forever Wars | Oracle Verification | Low |

The Liability State treats defense as a "Market," and like all markets, it requires aggressive anti-trust enforcement to prevent the "Vendor" from becoming the "Owner."
