# The Fiscal Gap

---

## The Risk: Structural Revenue Insufficiency

The core risk is that the Land Value Tax (LVT) and supplementary fees physically cannot generate enough revenue to sustain even a minimal state and its promised Citizen's Dividend.

---

## The Mechanism

### 1. The Math Gap
Current US land rental value is estimated at ~$1.2T/year, while the state requires significantly more to cover legacy debt, defense auditing, and a meaningful dividend.

### 2. Political Friction
The state must shrink by ~80% to fit within LVT revenue. The mechanism of failure is "Political Capture of the Dividend," where citizens demand programs that exceed the LVT cap, leading to debt and the return of income tax.

### 3. Revenue Elasticity
If LVT is set too high (100% of rental value), it may discourage efficient land use or lead to widespread abandonment of low-value land, shrinking the tax base.

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: Drastic State Reduction
Dismantle 80% of current bureaucracy, replacing all welfare with a single Dividend and all regulations with private insurance.

### Mitigation 2: Broad "Land" Definition
Expand LVT to include all monopoly rents: Electromagnetic spectrum, orbital slots, IP terms, and resource extraction rights.

### Mitigation 3: Floating Dividend
The Dividend is never a fixed dollar amount; it is (**Revenue - Costs**). This ensures the budget always balances by definition, preventing deficit spending.

---

## Organic Resolution

The Fiscal Gap is resolved not through legislative "shields," but through **Market Competition** and the **Subsistence Pivot**:
- **The Subsistence Pivot**: In a conventional state, revenue drops lead to debt. The Liability State has no credit rating; if revenue falls, the **Citizen's Dividend** automatically shrinks. This forces the collective to live within the "Natural Rent" of their soil.
- **Incentive Correction**: Citizens seeing their monthly dividend drop by $200 instantly become the most aggressive proponents of efficiency. They will vote to fire redundant auditors and open new "Land" (spectrum, mineral rights) for **Harberger taxation** to restore their income.
- **The LVT Feedback Loop**: Because the state is a **Zero-Sum Budget**, there is no incentive for the state to "grow" beyond what is profitable. If the state costs more than the value it provides, land values drop, revenue falls, and the state is forced to shrink or die. This aligns the proletariat and the state in the pursuit of **Systemic Profitability**.
