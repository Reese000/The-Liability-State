## The Bottom Line

The Liability State is **not** a utopia. It trades one set of problems for another:

| Current System Problems | Liability State Problems |
|------------------------|-------------------------|
| Bureaucratic capture | Insurance cartel capture |
| Slow adaptation | Asymmetric justice access |
| Moral hazard | Capital formation challenges |
| Democratic corruption | PMC loyalty risks |
| Regulatory capture | Algorithmic conformity |

**The Question**: Are the new problems smaller than the old ones? Are the mitigations sufficient?

**The Honest Answer**: Probably yes, but it requires:
- Constant vigilance against cartelization
- Strong constitutional protections
- Citizen engagement
- Willingness to adjust
