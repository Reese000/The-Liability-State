## Honest Assessment

The Liability State is a theoretical masterpiece of incentive alignment but an **operational minefield**. This section documents known vulnerabilities and proposed solutions, standardized into **Risk/Mechanism/Mitigation** profiles.

---

## The Core Paradox

The system solves the internal problems of the 20th-century state (bureaucracy, debt, moral hazard) by potentially introducing the problems of the 19th-century state (cartels, instability, financial panics) or the 21st-century tech-state (algorithmic tyranny).
