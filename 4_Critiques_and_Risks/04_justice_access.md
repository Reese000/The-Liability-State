# Justice Access (The Wealth Filter)

[← Back to Overview](./00_overview.md) | [Index](../README.md)

---

## The Risk: Plutocratic Adjudication

The risk is that a private, bond-based legal system prevents the poor from seeking justice and allows wealthy corporations to purchase favorable rulings.

---

## The Mechanism

### 1. The Bond Barrier
The requirement to post a "Litigation Bond" prevents those without liquid capital from filing legitimate suits, effectively granting immunity to those who harm the indigent.

### 2. Repeat Player Bias
Courts that depend on corporate "arbitration clauses" for revenue have a structural incentive to rule in favor of the corporations that write the contracts.

---

## Mitigation Strategies

### Mitigation 1: Trust Brokers (Litigation Finance)
Private firms that fund bonds for legitimate cases in exchange for a percentage of the payout. This turns "Justice for the Poor" into a profitable investment.

### Mitigation 2: Random Court Assignment
Mandate that courts for disputes are chosen randomly from a certified pool at the time of the dispute, preventing corporations from pre-selecting "friendly" venues in their contracts.

### Mitigation 3: Pro Bono Quotas
Certification for private courts requires a mandatory percentage of low-value or "Civil Rights" cases to be handled for free.

---

## Organic Resolution

The Wealth Filter in Justice is resolved through the **Securitization of Claims** and **Auditor Competition**:
- **Claims as Assets**: In a private court system, a clear liability claim against a wealthy defendant is a **High-Value Financial Asset**. A poor victim can sell a "Participation Interest" in their lawsuit to a **Trust Broker** (speculator) for immediate cash to cover the surety bond.
- **The Liability Scalp**: Trust Brokers are incentivized to find and fund these cases because the "Profit" from a successful judgment against a negligent plutocrat is massive. This turns the poor into "Bounty Hunters" of corporate negligence.
- **Adversarial Courts**: Because DROs (Private Courts) compete on reputation, any court that consistently rules in favor of the rich loses its **Insurance Certification**. No insurer will recognize the rulings of a biased court, making that court's "Judgment" worthless in the global trust market.

## Bottom Line

| Risk | Mitigation | Residual Risk |
|------|------------|---------------|
| Bond Barrier | Trust Brokers | Medium |
| Court Bias | Random Assignment | Low |
| Civil Rights Gap | specialized Insurance | Medium |

Justice in the Liability State is a commodity, but "Trust Brokers" ensure that even the poor can "sell" their high-quality claims to the highest bidder.
