2. The Solution: The "Feebate" (Revenue Neutrality)
To use incentive taxation safely in the Liability State, you must use a Closed-Loop System called a "Feebate."

In a Feebate, the government never spends a dime of its own money. It simply redistributes money from "Bad Actors" to "Good Actors" within the same industry.

Example (Cars):

You don't give a tax credit for electric cars (which drains the treasury).

Instead, you set a standard: "30 MPG."

The Fee: If you buy a gas guzzler (15 MPG), you pay a $2,000 fee at the dealership.

The Rebate: That $2,000 is immediately given to the guy buying the high-efficiency car.

Why this works:

Self-Funding: It costs the State $0.

Dynamic: If everyone starts buying efficient cars, the fees dry up, but so do the rebates. The system naturally turns itself off as the goal is achieved.
