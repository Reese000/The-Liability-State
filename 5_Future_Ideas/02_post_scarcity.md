## The "Wall-E" Problem

### The Question

What is the average human for when robots do all the work?

### The Risk

If life is too easy, humans become:
- Passive consumers
- Purposeless
- Depressed
- Socially atomized

---

## The Liability State Answer

### Built-In Purpose Mechanisms

| Mechanism | How It Provides Purpose |
|-----------|------------------------|
| **Guild Mastery** | Status from expertise, not birth |
| **Masterpiece Requirement** | Must prove yourself |
| **Reputation Capital** | "What have you built?" |
| **Frontiers** | Physical struggle for those who want it |
| **Human Premium** | What machines can't do = highest value |

## The Human Premium Economy

### What Machines Can't Do

| Human Trait | Economic Value |
|-------------|----------------|
| **Empathy** | Companionship, therapy, care |
| **Judgment** | Subjective evaluation, art criticism |
| **Presence** | Being there in person |
| **Context** | Understanding unstated needs |
| **Creativity** | Novel combination of ideas |
| **Risk-Taking** | Willingness to fail personally |

### Examples

| Role | Why Human |
|------|-----------|
| Bartender | Takes 2 minutes longer, but conversation is the product |
| Teacher | Mentorship, inspiration, role modeling |
| Companion | Elderly need human connection |
| Beta Tester | Subjective experience feedback |
| Artisan | "Made by human hands" = luxury |

## The Dividend Economy

### How It Evolves

As automation increases:

1. **Production costs** → Zero
2. **Land/resource values** → Increase
3. **LVT revenue** → Increases
4. **Citizen's Dividend** → Increases
5. **Work necessity** → Decreases

### The Choice Economy

Eventually, people **choose** to work:
- For status
- For community
- For purpose
- For challenge

Not for survival.

## Challenges

### The Restless Problem

Some people need struggle:
- Competition is biologically satisfying
- Risk provides meaning
- Physical challenge builds identity

**Solution**: Frontiers—space, wilderness, extreme environments.

### The Meaning Problem

| Risk | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Depression | No external structure |
| Addiction | Easy escape from emptiness |
| Nihilism | No obvious purpose |

**Solution**: Guilds provide structure, community, progression.
