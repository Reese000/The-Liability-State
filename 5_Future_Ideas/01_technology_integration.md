## AI in the Liability State

### AI as Auditor

Current proposal uses humans for auditing. AI could enhance:

| Function | AI Enhancement |
|----------|---------------|
| **Court Records** | AI searches precedent instantly |
| **Fraud Detection** | Pattern recognition across claims |
| **Insurance Pricing** | Real-time risk assessment |
| **Government Audit** | Automated budget verification |

### AI Liability Question

**When AI causes harm, who is liable?**

| Scenario | Current Approach | Liability State Approach |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------------|
| Self-driving car crash | Manufacturer | Manufacturer + deployer + developer |
| AI medical misdiagnosis | Unclear | Clear liability chain to humans |
| Algorithmic discrimination | Fines | Personal liability for executives |

**Principle**: Liability always traces back to human decision-makers.

## Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs)

### The Problem

How to audit government secrets without revealing them?

### The Solution

ZKPs allow proving statements without revealing underlying data:

| Statement to Prove | What's Revealed |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| "We spent $1B on fuel" | Nothing about fuel formula |
| "Budget is balanced" | No individual line items |
| "No unauthorized troops" | No troop locations |
| "Tax collected correctly" | No individual assessments |

**Application**: Audit the secret state without compromising security.

## Time-Lock Encryption

### The Problem

Secrets are classified forever; no accountability.

### The Solution

Time-lock encryption mathematically guarantees auto-release:

| Classification Level | Auto-Release |
|---------------------|--------------|
| Tactical operations | 1 year |
| Strategic decisions | 5 years |
| Political negotiations | 10 years |
| Lifetime secrets | Death of last participant |

**Effect**: Leaders won't commit crimes knowing evidence will emerge.
