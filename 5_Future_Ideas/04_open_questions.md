# Open Questions

## Areas Needing Further Development

This document catalogs questions that remain unresolved or underexplored.

---

## Economic Questions

### Revenue Sufficiency

- Can LVT + pollution fees actually close the fiscal gap?
- What happens during extended economic downturns?
- How do we handle transition-period deficits?

### Investment Dynamics

- Will bounties actually attract sufficient R&D investment?
- How do we price insurance for genuinely unprecedented risks?
- What prevents a perpetual "slow economy" under unlimited liability?

### Inheritance Tax Mechanics

- How do we value non-liquid businesses for equity payment?
- What prevents gaming through trusts and offshore structures?
- How do we handle cross-border inheritance?

---

## Legal Questions

### Court Certification

- What specific standards constitute "due process"?
- How do we prevent regulatory capture of certification bodies?
- What happens when courts disagree on precedent?

### International Jurisdiction

- How do cross-border disputes work?
- What if other nations don't recognize our judgments?
- How do we extradite judgment-evaders?

### Criminal Law

- What role does the state play in prosecution?
- Are there any crimes beyond liability (true crimes vs. torts)?
- How is murder handled? Imprisonment?

---

## Political Questions

### Transition Authority

- Who has authority during the Regency Period?
- How do we prevent the "never-ending transition"?
- What happens if transition fails midway?

### Democratic Participation

- Is sortition alone sufficient for legitimacy?
- How do citizens influence policy beyond referendums?
- What prevents tyranny of apathy?

### Regional Compacts

- What if a region wants to leave the Alliance?
- How do we handle vastly different regional cultures?
- What's the minimum common standard?

---

## Defense Questions

### Existential Threats

- What if the enemy is willing to accept any cost?
- How do we handle nuclear or biological threats?
- What about threats that can't be priced (e.g., AI)?

### PMC Coordination

- Who commands during active conflict?
- How do we prevent friendly fire between competitors?
- What's the chain of command?

### Occupation Recovery

- If we're conquered, how does the system reconstitute?
- Where are backups of legal records stored?
- How do we prevent total cultural erasure?

---

## Cultural Questions

### Guild Formation

- How do new guilds form?
- What if guilds become exclusionary?
- How do we prevent guild monopolies?

### Education Standards

- What's the minimum education before adulthood?
- How do we ensure literacy/numeracy?
- Who certifies the certifiers?

### Family Law

- How are children protected?
- What happens in custody disputes?
- How is child abuse handled (crime or tort)?

---

## Technology Questions

### AI Liability

- When AI is autonomous, whose liability is it?
- What about emergent behaviors no one predicted?
- How do we handle AI-on-AI conflicts?

### Biotech Ethics

- What enhancements are permitted?
- How do we handle genetic modification liability?
- What about longevity treatments and inheritance?

### Digital Identity

- Who controls the identity infrastructure?
- What happens if identity is stolen?
- How do we handle identity for AI entities?

---

## Philosophical Questions

### Human Nature

- Is the system too optimistic about human rationality?
- Does everyone respond to incentives equally?
- What about the truly irrational?

### Meaning and Purpose

- Can guilds really replace religion/ideology?
- What if people don't find the Human Premium satisfying?
- How do we handle mass depression/nihilism?

### The Unknowable

- What if a Black Swan event breaks all assumptions?
- How does the system handle unprecedented categories of harm?
- What's the ultimate fallback?

## Call to Action

These questions need:
- More detailed analysis
- Historical case studies
- Game-theoretic modeling
- Pilot program data
- Ongoing revision as conditions change

The Liability State is not a finished system—it's a framework requiring continuous refinement.
