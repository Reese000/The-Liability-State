## The Long-Term Vision

### Phase 1: Establishment (2030-2050)
- Transition complete
- System operating as designed
- Dividends replacing welfare
- Private courts established

### Phase 2: Optimization (2050-2080)
- AI integration deepens
- Automation increases dividend
- Guild system mature
- Space colonization begins

### Phase 3: Post-Scarcity (2080+)
- Work becomes optional
- Status from contribution, not wealth
- Human premium dominant economy
- Multi-planetary civilization
