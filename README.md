## The One-Paragraph Summary

The Liability State replaces government regulation with strict liability enforced through private courts and mandatory insurance. Revenue comes from Land Value Tax (100% of rental value) and pollution fees—no income tax. Citizens receive a Universal Dividend from surplus revenue. Corporations operate without limited liability shields; executives and shareholders are personally liable for harms. The political structure consists of a weak "Strategist" (proposes national missions but controls no money) and a "House of Repeal" (randomly selected citizens who can only remove laws, not create them). Defense is handled by competing private military companies, kept in check through escrowed munitions, surety bonds, and an armed citizen militia.

## Key Terminology

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Ex-Ante** | Before the fact (preventative regulation) |
| **Ex-Post** | After the fact (accountability via liability) |
| **LVT** | Land Value Tax — 100% levy on rental value of land |
|| **Citizen's Dividend** | UBI funded by LVT surplus — a fixed national amount pegged to subsistence in the cheapest habitable area |
| **The Strategist** | Executive with influence but no authority over treasury |
| **House of Repeal** | Legislature that can only remove laws, not create them |
| **Sortition** | Random selection of citizens (like jury duty) |
|| **Regional Compact** | State-level cultural "operating system" |
|| **Trust Market** | Ecosystem of private courts and certifiers |
|| **Private Civil Servant (PCS)** | Deep-access auditor who voluntarily accepts extreme personal liability in exchange for warrant-based access to corporate secrets |
|| **Supreme Trust Oracle** | Backstop enforcement layer at the Alliance level — handles inter-compact disputes that private courts cannot resolve (Trust Blockade, force prohibition enforcement) |
|| **Good Faith Bond** | 10% of compact LVT revenue escrowed at the Alliance level to cover adverse STO rulings |
|| **Glass Box** | Transparency mandate: all corporate and government operations are publicly auditable |
|| **Feebate** | Revenue-neutral tax that shifts money from bad to good actors |
| **Bounty War** | Defense through per-unit prizes for neutralizing threats |
| **Escrowed Munitions** | Heavy weapons locked until court-authorized conflict |
