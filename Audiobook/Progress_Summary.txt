# THE LIABILITY STATE - PROGRESS SUMMARY
# Condensed version (~15 min) with all systemic corrections

---

## CORE CONCEPT (2 min)

The Liability State replaces bureaucratic regulation with priced liability. If your actions harm others, insurance pays immediately—then your premiums rise.

Three pillars:
1. **LVT** funds government via Budget-First Cap (not 100% extraction)
2. **Insurance buffer** replaces regulation (graduated consequences, not instant ruin)
3. **Constitutional Lock** prevents class warfare (75% supermajority to change tax rate)

---

## ECONOMIC SYSTEM (3 min)

**Revenue**: Land Value Tax using Budget-First formula:
```
LVT Rate = (Defense + Courts + Subsistence Floor) / Total Land Value
```

**Harberger Self-Assessment**: Owners declare property value. Anyone can buy at that price. Defensive Ratchet: refuse sale → assessment ratchets up. No bureaucrat assessors.

**Dividend**: Subsistence floor, not luxury. Pegged to cheapest 10% of counties. Covers survival in rural areas; requires work in cities.

**Corporate Liability**: Graduated, not punitive.
- Accident → Insurance pays, premiums rise
- Repeated negligence → Unaffordable premiums, market exit
- Calculated negligence → Veil pierced, executives lose personal assets

---

## LEGAL SYSTEM (2 min)

**Insurance as Buffer**: Victims paid immediately. Companies face premium consequences, not instant bankruptcy.

**Uninsurable Cliff**: Only chronic or calculated negligence triggers policy cancellation → cannot operate legally.

**Loser Pays + Litigation Bonds**: Filter frivolous suits.

---

## POLITICAL STRUCTURE (2 min)

**Strategist**: Vision, no authority. Cannot access treasury or police.

**House of Repeal**: Random lottery selection. Can only remove laws, not add.

**Constitutional Lock**: LVT rate locked. 75% supermajority to change.

**Zero-Sum Budget**: Revenue - Costs = Dividend. Want more programs? Smaller dividend. Want bigger dividend? Smaller government. Citizens police waste.

---

## DISABILITY CARE (1 min)

**Genetic Lottery Pool**: No bureaucrat gatekeeping.
- Fixed % of LVT funds High-Risk Reinsurance Pool
- Three random independent physicians diagnose
- Consensus → coverage triggers automatically
- It's a contract, not a favor

---

## CLASS WARFARE PREVENTION (2 min)

**Constitutional Lock**: Tax rate requires 75% supermajority
**Zero-Sum Budget**: Spending comes from citizen dividends
**Harberger Self-Assessment**: No corrupt assessors
**Defensive Ratchet**: Protection from hostile buyouts
**Subsistence Floor**: Dividend = survival, not luxury

---

## KEY RISKS & MITIGATIONS (2 min)

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Fiscal Gap | Budget-First Cap (collect only what's needed) |
| Insurance Cartel | Antitrust, mutual cooperatives, foreign competition |
| Class Warfare | Constitutional Lock + Zero-Sum Budget |
| Justice Access | Trust Brokers, litigation insurance, Guild coverage |
| Military Coup | Fragmentation, escrowed munitions, citizen militia |
| Harassment Buyouts | Defensive Ratchet |

---

## THE BET

The Liability State bets that:
- Insurance as buffer works better than captured regulators
- Harberger Tax forces honest valuation without bureaucracy
- Subsistence floor keeps labor market hungry while preventing starvation
- Constitutional Lock prevents democratic theft
- Graduated liability punishes negligence without destroying honest businesses
