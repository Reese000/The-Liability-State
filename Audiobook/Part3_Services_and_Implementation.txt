# THE LIABILITY STATE AUDIOBOOK
# Part 3: Social Services, Taxation, and Implementation

---

## Chapter 12: Infrastructure

Roads operate as subscription services with Pothole Guarantees. If a pothole damages your vehicle, the road owner is strictly liable. This makes road owners obsessed with maintenance.

What about getting landlocked? Common Law provides Easements of Necessity. Every property has inherent Right of Way.

Property rights extend to peace on your property. If your neighbor blasts music at two AM, you bill them for Loss of Quiet Enjoyment.

Public spaces still exist but are structured differently. Parks and wildlands are owned by Private Trusts. Trustees have fiduciary duty to maintain them.

## Chapter 13: Social Services

**The Citizen's Dividend** is a subsistence floor, not a salary.

The Dividend is calculated to provide two thousand calories per day and basic shelter—in the lowest-cost ten percent of counties. It protects you from starvation, not from labor.

In rural areas, the dividend covers rent, food, and utilities. In expensive cities, it covers almost nothing. Want city life? You must work.

This creates the "Hunger Gap" incentive. People work because they want comfort. But no one starves.

**Disability Care** uses the Genetic Lottery Pool—not bureaucrat gatekeeping.

For accident-caused disability, insurance pays immediately. Tort settlements cover lifetime care.

For congenital disability, the system works differently. A fixed percentage of LVT revenue goes to a High-Risk Reinsurance Pool. Three independent, randomly-selected physicians diagnose the patient. If all three concur on a diagnosis code, coverage activates automatically.

This is a contract, not a favor. No caseworker decides your fate. Colluding doctors face fraud liability and license revocation.

**Guild Safety Nets** provide fraternal benefits. Your Machinists' Guild covers you when sick. Guild brothers know you personally—they know if you're faking.

**Education** uses vouchers that follow children to Micro-Schools. Graduation requires a Masterpiece—demonstrated skill, not attendance.

## Chapter 14: Taxation

Taxation in the Liability State steers behavior rather than just raising revenue.

**The Budget-First Cap.** The LVT rate is not one hundred percent extraction. It is calculated as Defense plus Courts plus Subsistence Floor, divided by Total Land Value.

If government shrinks, taxes drop automatically. The rate is constitutionally locked.

**The Harberger Tax** eliminates bureaucratic assessors. Owners declare their own property value. They pay tax on that number. But anyone can buy at their declared price.

The Defensive Ratchet prevents griefing: you can refuse any sale, but your assessment ratchets up to the offer price. This forces honest valuation without bureaucracy.

**Feebates** are closed-loop incentive systems. Buy a gas guzzler—pay a two-thousand-dollar fee. That fee immediately funds a rebate for the buyer of an efficient car. The government spends nothing.

**Inheritance Tax** aligns with meritocracy. Large estates face high taxes. But heirs can pay in equity—keeping control while dividends flow to the public.

**The Tobin Tax** puts a tiny levy on financial transactions. Long-term investors barely notice. High-frequency algorithms have their margins destroyed.

# PART THREE: IMPLEMENTATION

## Chapter 15: The Transition Challenge

You cannot switch overnight. The solution is the Regency Period—a ten to twenty year transition.

**Phase One**: Years one through five. Certify first Private Courts. Launch LVT pilot zones. Create liability insurance markets.

**Phase Two**: Years five through ten. Private courts handle fifty percent of cases. LVT covers thirty percent of municipal revenue. Income tax rates begin declining.

**Phase Three**: Years ten through fifteen. Private courts become primary judiciary. LVT becomes primary revenue.

**Phase Four**: Years fifteen through twenty. Government reduced to core functions. Full Liability State achieved.

## Chapter 16: Applying to Modern America

The target structure replaces the current four-layer system with two layers: Federal Auditor and Municipal Corporations, with Regional Compacts providing cultural flavor.

At the federal level, what remains: Defense audits. The National Trust manages LVT revenue. Currency is maintained. The Strategist proposes missions.

What dissolves: The IRS becomes simple LVT collection. The EPA becomes a pollution fee collector. The FDA shifts to insurance and certification. Most cabinet departments have functions privatized.

Key constitutional amendments would be required. The Liability Clause: no one shielded from liability by corporate structure. The Land Value Clause: no tax on income. The Negative Legislature: the House can only repeal. The Constitutional Lock: LVT rate requires seventy-five percent supermajority.
